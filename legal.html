Privacy

Kurds for Christ will respect your privacy and handle personal data in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We will only collect the information we need, explain why we need it, and never sell your data.

This prototype

This private preview does not collect, send or store any personal information. Forms and donation screens are demonstrations only. Web fonts and icons are loaded from third-party content delivery networks (Google Fonts and unpkg), which may see your IP address.

Cookies

This prototype sets no cookies. It uses your browser’s session storage only to remember whether you hid the preview banner or chose a language preview; this never leaves your device. The public website will include a cookie notice if any non-essential cookies are used.

Safeguarding

Kurds for Christ is committed to safeguarding children and adults at risk in all of its activities, in the UK and internationally. A full safeguarding policy, a designated safeguarding lead and reporting procedures will be in place before any programme begins.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, call 999 in the UK, or local emergency services elsewhere.

Terms

Content on this prototype is for review by the founding trustees only and is not for public distribution. Imagery is illustrative, and sample stories and articles are not real. No donations can be made through this preview.
