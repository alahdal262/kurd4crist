## Vision
# KURDS FOR CHRIST

**Faith • Community • Heritage • Hope**

Kurds for Christ is a proposed UK-based Christian charitable organisation headquartered in Liverpool, England, with an international vision to serve Kurdish Christians and Kurdish Christian communities around the world.

The organisation exists to advance the Christian faith, strengthen Kurdish Christian communities, support missionaries and churches, provide humanitarian and community assistance, advance education and skills, and preserve Kurdish Christian identity, language and heritage.

Kurds for Christ seeks to combine Christian faith with practical service. We believe that Christian ministry includes both sharing the message of Jesus Christ and serving people through compassion, education, relief, community support and opportunities that help individuals and families build stronger futures.

Our long-term vision is to develop a trusted international network connecting Kurdish Christians in the United Kingdom, Europe, the Middle East, the United States and other parts of the world.

## Our Vision

Our vision is to see Kurdish Christians strengthened spiritually, socially and practically, connected across borders, supported in their faith, and equipped to serve their families, churches and communities.

We aim to create a global network where Kurdish Christians can find fellowship, Christian teaching, practical support, educational opportunities, humanitarian assistance and meaningful connections with other believers.

We also seek to preserve and strengthen Kurdish Christian heritage for future generations.

## Our Mission

Kurds for Christ exists to serve Jesus Christ by advancing the Christian faith and supporting Kurdish Christian communities worldwide.

Our mission includes:

- sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ;
- supporting Christian discipleship and spiritual growth;
- supporting missionaries, pastors and Christian workers;
- strengthening Kurdish Christian churches and fellowships;
- creating Christian resources in Kurdish and other relevant languages;
- providing humanitarian relief and practical assistance;
- supporting education, skills and community development;
- strengthening families and individuals experiencing hardship;
- creating opportunities for Kurdish Christians to connect and serve together;
- preserving Kurdish Christian culture, language and heritage;
- supporting the establishment, development, refurbishment and equipping of churches and places of Christian worship where lawful and appropriate.

## Christian Faith and Mission

Christian faith is at the heart of Kurds for Christ.

The organisation seeks to advance Christianity through activities including:

Christian evangelism, Bible teaching, prayer, worship, discipleship, Christian fellowship, digital mission, Christian media, distribution of Bibles and Christian resources, missionary support, leadership development and church support.

We want Kurdish Christians to have access to high-quality Christian teaching and resources in languages they understand, particularly Kurdish Kurmanji and Kurdish Sorani.

We also aim to support the development of Kurdish Christian digital media, including videos, podcasts, online Bible teaching, testimonies, worship content and Christian educational resources.

## Supporting Missionaries

Kurds for Christ aims to establish a structured missionary-support programme.

This may include supporting missionaries, pastors, evangelists and Christian workers through:

- training;
- ministry resources;
- equipment;
- approved project costs;
- Christian literature;
- travel associated with legitimate charitable mission;
- leadership development;
- church and community ministry;
- financial grants where appropriate.

All financial support will be subject to appropriate due diligence, financial controls, safeguarding, monitoring and reporting.

## Supporting Churches

Kurds for Christ seeks to strengthen Kurdish Christian churches and Christian communities.

Our long-term work may include:

- supporting existing churches;
- supporting Kurdish Christian fellowships;
- church planting;
- leadership and ministry training;
- purchasing or providing Christian resources;
- church equipment;
- refurbishment of churches;
- development of community facilities connected with churches;
- support for Christian youth and family programmes;
- and, where legally permitted and properly governed, helping to establish or construct places of Christian worship.

International church projects will only be undertaken following appropriate legal, financial, safeguarding and sanctions checks.

## Community Support

Kurds for Christ believes Christian service should also respond to practical human needs.

Alongside our faith and mission programmes, we aim to develop charitable community programmes that may include:

- poverty relief;
- support for families experiencing hardship;
- refugee and migrant support;
- community integration;
- employment support;
- English-language learning;
- digital skills;
- vocational training;
- mentoring;
- volunteering;
- social inclusion;
- wellbeing activities;
- support for people experiencing isolation;
- education and community development.

Where programmes are funded through non-religious grants, they will be delivered according to the conditions of the funder and will remain appropriately separated from religious activities.

## Education and Skills

Education and empowerment will form an important part of our community work.

Future programmes may include:

- English language and conversation classes;
- digital skills;
- artificial intelligence and technology literacy;
- employability training;
- CV and interview preparation;
- business and entrepreneurship skills;
- financial literacy;
- self-employment training;
- vocational education;
- mentoring and leadership development.

Our aim is not only to provide short-term assistance but also to help people develop the knowledge, skills and confidence needed to build sustainable futures.

## Humanitarian Relief

Kurds for Christ aims to support Kurdish Christian communities and other eligible beneficiaries facing poverty, displacement, conflict or humanitarian crisis.

Potential assistance may include:

- essential food;
- clothing;
- hygiene supplies;
- emergency support;
- household essentials;
- educational support;
- livelihood assistance;
- humanitarian grants;
- community recovery projects.

International humanitarian projects will be subject to strict due diligence, sanctions screening, financial controls and monitoring.

## Kurdish Christian Heritage

Kurds for Christ also seeks to preserve and strengthen Kurdish Christian identity and heritage.

This may include:

- Kurdish language initiatives;
- Christian resources in Kurdish;
- Kurdish Christian history;
- oral histories and testimonies;
- music and worship;
- cultural and educational activities;
- intergenerational programmes;
- digital archives and media;
- connecting Kurdish Christians in the homeland and diaspora.

The organisation is not affiliated with any political party, armed organisation or political movement.

Its Kurdish identity is cultural, community-focused and Christian rather than party-political.

## Our Geographic Vision

Kurds for Christ is intended to be headquartered in Liverpool, England, while developing an international network.

Our long-term vision may include partnerships and charitable activities among Kurdish Christian communities in:

- the United Kingdom;
- Europe;
- Iraq;
- the Kurdistan Region of Iraq;
- Syria;
- Kurdish communities in Syria;
- the United States;
- and other countries where legitimate charitable needs exist.

International work will be developed gradually and responsibly, with appropriate local partnerships and governance.

## Our Values

### Christ-Centred

Jesus Christ is at the heart of our Christian mission and identity.

### Faith

We believe faith should be expressed through both words and actions.

### Compassion

We seek to respond to need with practical love and mercy.

### Dignity

We believe every person should be treated with dignity and respect.

### Hope

We seek to bring spiritual and practical hope to individuals, families and communities.

### Integrity

We are committed to responsible, transparent and ethical management.

### Accountability

Funds and projects should be properly recorded, monitored and reported.

### Service

Leadership exists to serve people rather than to create personal benefit.

### Heritage

We value and preserve Kurdish language, culture, identity and Christian heritage.

### Partnership

We seek to work with churches, charities, community organisations, volunteers and responsible international partners.

## Governance

Kurds for Christ is intended to be established in England and Wales as a Charitable Incorporated Organisation.

The founding trustees are:

**Manan Misho – Trustee**

**Eyad Hakeem – Trustee**

The trustees will be collectively responsible for the charity's governance, finances, safeguarding, strategy and compliance.

The organisation will maintain clear financial separation between different funding streams, particularly between:

- Christian faith and mission funding;
- community and civil-grant funding;
- humanitarian funding;
- restricted grants;
- missionary-support funds;
- church-development funds.

## Funding

Kurds for Christ intends to develop a diverse and sustainable funding base.

Potential funding sources include:

- individual donations;
- Christian donors;
- churches;
- Christian trusts and foundations;
- missionary organisations;
- charitable foundations;
- community grants;
- institutional grants;
- fundraising activities;
- international Christian partners;
- eligible public and community funding programmes.

Funding received for a restricted purpose will only be used for that purpose.

Community grants will not be used for religious activities where the funder's rules prohibit this.

## Our Long-Term Vision

Our ambition is to develop Kurds for Christ into a trusted international Kurdish Christian charitable network.

Over time, we hope to establish:

- a strong registered charity in the United Kingdom;
- a professional management and governance structure;
- Kurdish Christian fellowships and networks;
- Christian digital media platforms;
- Kurdish-language Christian resources;
- missionary-support programmes;
- church-support programmes;
- humanitarian and relief projects;
- education and skills programmes;
- community-development projects;
- partnerships across Europe, the Middle East and North America;
- and, when appropriate, legally independent partner organisations in other countries.

## Who We Are in One Statement
