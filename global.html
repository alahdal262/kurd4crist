Our principles

How we will work internationally

  • Local partnerships with churches and organisations that share our values
  • Appropriate legal, financial, safeguarding and sanctions checks before any project
  • Clear governance and reporting to the trustees in the UK
  • No affiliation with any political party, armed organisation or political movement
  • Respect for local laws and for the dignity of every community we serve
