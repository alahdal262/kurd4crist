Faith & Mission

Sharing the Hope of Jesus Christ

Christian faith is at the heart of Kurds for Christ. We want every Kurdish Christian to have access to Scripture, teaching, fellowship and worship in a language they understand.

Mission Partners

In our heart language

Christian resources in Kurmanji and Sorani

High-quality Christian teaching should be available in the languages Kurdish families speak at home.

  • Bible reading plans and study guides
  • Worship songs and lyrics
  • Video teaching with subtitles
  • Children’s and youth discipleship materials
  • Audio resources for listeners who prefer spoken content
