Kurdish Christian Heritage

Preserving Faith. Preserving Heritage.

Kurdish Christian identity is a living inheritance, carried in language, song, memory and faith. We want to honour it, record it and pass it on to the next generation.

Intergenerational

From grandparents to grandchildren

Many Kurdish Christian families carry memories that have never been written down. Our heritage programme will help families record them, in their own words and their own language.

  • Recording workshops for families and churches
  • Young volunteers interviewing elders
  • Bilingual transcripts in Kurmanji, Sorani and English
  • Respectful consent and clear control over what is shared
