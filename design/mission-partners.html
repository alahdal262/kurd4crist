How it works

A clear, fair process

Apply → Review → Due Diligence → Partnership → Support → Reporting. A transparent process protects mission partners, beneficiaries and donors alike.

  1. Apply

    Mission partners share their calling, ministry, church connection and the support they are seeking.

  2. Review

    Applications are reviewed against our charitable objects and priorities by a trustee-approved panel.

  3. Due Diligence

    Identity, references, safeguarding, financial and sanctions checks, proportionate to the support requested.

  4. Partnership

    A written agreement sets out purpose, budget, safeguarding expectations and reporting.

  5. Support

    Approved resources, training or grants are provided, in stages where appropriate.

  6. Reporting

    Partners report on activity and spending; we monitor, learn and report to supporters.

  • Training
  • Ministry resources
  • Equipment
  • Approved project costs
  • Christian literature
  • Travel associated with legitimate charitable mission
  • Leadership development
  • Church and community ministry
  • Financial grants where appropriate

Safeguards

Every gift handled with care

All financial support will be subject to appropriate due diligence, financial controls, safeguarding, monitoring and reporting.

Due diligence

Identity, references and ministry verification before any commitment.

Safeguarding

Partners agree to safeguarding standards for children and adults at risk.

Sanctions & controls

Sanctions screening and financial controls for any international transfer.

Monitoring

Staged funding, receipts and regular reporting to the trustees.

Stand with a mission partner

Gifts to the Missionary Support fund are restricted and used only for approved mission-partner support.
