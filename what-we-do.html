How we work

Principles behind every programme

Funding received for a restricted purpose will only be used for that purpose. Community grants will not be used for religious activities where the funder’s rules prohibit this.

Separate funding streams

Faith, community, humanitarian, missionary-support and church-development funds are recorded and reported separately.

Safeguarding & due diligence

Every partnership and programme is checked for safeguarding, financial and sanctions risk before it begins.

Grow gradually

We will start in Liverpool and grow responsibly, with local partners and proper governance.
