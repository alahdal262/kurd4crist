Governance, safeguarding and financial accountability

Governance

Intended to be established in England and Wales as a Charitable Incorporated Organisation (CIO), with a written constitution, regular trustee meetings and minuted decisions.

Safeguarding

Safeguarding children and adults at risk is a trustee responsibility. A designated safeguarding lead, policy and training will be in place before any programme begins.

Safeguarding statement

Financial Accountability

Funds and projects will be properly recorded, monitored and reported, with dual authorisation for payments and independent examination of accounts.

Restricted Funds

Clear separation between funding streams

Funding received for a restricted purpose will only be used for that purpose. Community grants will not be used for religious activities where the funder’s rules prohibit this.
