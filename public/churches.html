Families & young people

Churches at the heart of community life

Healthy churches serve their neighbours. We hope to support Christian youth and family programmes connected with Kurdish churches and fellowships.

  • Youth discipleship and mentoring
  • Family and marriage support
  • Children’s ministry resources, with safeguarding training
  • Community meals and hospitality

Responsible development

Checks before any international church project

International church projects will only be undertaken following appropriate legal, financial, safeguarding and sanctions checks.

Legal

Confirming that activity is lawful in the country concerned, including land and building permissions.

Financial

Budgets, quotes, staged payments and independent verification of costs.

Safeguarding

Partner safeguarding policies and training in place before support begins.

Sanctions

Screening of partners and payment routes against UK and international sanctions.
