Community

Practical support for stronger lives and communities

Christian service should respond to real human needs. Our community programmes will offer welcome, learning and practical help to people who need it, starting here in Liverpool.

Ask about supportVolunteer with us

Support based on charitable need.

Community support is offered on the basis of charitable need and is open to people of all backgrounds and beliefs. No one is required to take part in religious activity to receive help.

Clear separation of funding.

Where programmes are funded through non-religious grants, they will be delivered according to the funder’s conditions and kept appropriately separate from religious activities.

Education & Skills

Knowledge, skills and confidence for sustainable futures

Our aim is not only to provide short-term assistance, but to help people build the knowledge, skills and confidence they need for the long term.

  • English language and conversation classes
  • Digital skills
  • Artificial intelligence and technology literacy
  • Employability training
  • CV and interview preparation
  • Business and entrepreneurship skills
  • Financial literacy
  • Self-employment training
  • Vocational education
  • Mentoring and leadership development

How it works

Simple, respectful, practical

We will keep access to support straightforward and dignified.

  1. Get in touch

    Contact us or speak to a volunteer at a community session.

  2. A friendly conversation

    We listen and understand what support would help.

  3. A simple plan

    Together we agree next steps, classes or referrals.

  4. Follow-up

    We check in and celebrate progress along the way.
