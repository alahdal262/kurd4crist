Our mission

Kurds for Christ exists to serve Jesus Christ by advancing the Christian faith and strengthening Kurdish Christian communities worldwide.

We believe Christian ministry includes both sharing the message of Jesus Christ and serving people through compassion, education, relief and opportunities that help individuals and families build stronger futures.
