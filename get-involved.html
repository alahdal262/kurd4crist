Pray with us

Prayer focus

Prayer is the foundation of our work. Please pray with us:

  1. For Kurdish Christians to be strengthened in faith, hope and love.
  2. For pastors, missionaries and Christian workers serving Kurdish communities.
  3. For families facing hardship, displacement or isolation.
  4. For Kurdish Christian resources, translation and digital mission.
  5. For wisdom for our trustees as Kurds for Christ is established.
  6. For peace, dignity and flourishing for all people in the region.
