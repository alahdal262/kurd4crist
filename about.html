About us

A Kurdish Christian charity, rooted in Liverpool, serving across borders.

Kurds for Christ is a proposed UK-based Christian charitable organisation that exists to advance the Christian faith, strengthen Kurdish Christian communities and serve people through compassionate charitable action.

Who we are

Christian faith and practical service, together.

Kurds for Christ is intended to be headquartered in Liverpool, England, with an international vision to serve Kurdish Christians and Kurdish Christian communities around the world.

The organisation exists to advance the Christian faith, strengthen Kurdish Christian communities, support missionaries and churches, provide humanitarian and community assistance, advance education and skills, and preserve Kurdish Christian identity, language and heritage.

We believe that Christian ministry includes both sharing the message of Jesus Christ and serving people through compassion, education, relief, community support and opportunities that help individuals and families build stronger futures.

Our vision

Strengthened, connected and equipped to serve.

Our vision is to see Kurdish Christians strengthened spiritually, socially and practically, connected across borders, supported in their faith, and equipped to serve their families, churches and communities.

We aim to create a global network where Kurdish Christians can find fellowship, Christian teaching, practical support, educational opportunities, humanitarian assistance and meaningful connections with other believers, and to preserve Kurdish Christian heritage for future generations.

Our mission

To serve Jesus Christ by advancing the Christian faith and supporting Kurdish Christian communities worldwide.

Our mission includes:

  • Sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ
  • Supporting Christian discipleship and spiritual growth
  • Supporting missionaries, pastors and Christian workers
  • Strengthening Kurdish Christian churches and fellowships
  • Creating Christian resources in Kurdish and other relevant languages
  • Providing humanitarian relief and practical assistance
  • Supporting education, skills and community development
  • Strengthening families and individuals experiencing hardship
  • Creating opportunities for Kurdish Christians to connect and serve together
  • Preserving Kurdish Christian culture, language and heritage
  • Supporting the establishment, development, refurbishment and equipping of churches and places of Christian worship where lawful and appropriate

Our Christian faith

Christ at the heart of our identity.

Christian faith is at the heart of Kurds for Christ. We seek to advance Christianity through evangelism, Bible teaching, prayer, worship, discipleship, fellowship, digital mission and the distribution of Bibles and Christian resources.

We want Kurdish Christians to have access to high-quality Christian teaching and resources in languages they understand, particularly Kurdish Kurmanji and Kurdish Sorani.

Our Kurdish heritage

Cultural, community-focused and Christian. Never party-political.

We value and preserve Kurdish language, culture, identity and Christian heritage, and seek to connect Kurdish Christians in the homeland and the diaspora.

Political independence. Kurds for Christ is not affiliated with any political party, armed organisation or political movement. Its Kurdish identity is cultural, community-focused and Christian rather than party-political.

Our governance

Accountable stewardship from day one.

Kurds for Christ is intended to be established in England and Wales as a Charitable Incorporated Organisation (CIO). The founding trustees, Jamila Bilal (Chair), Manan Misho and Eyad Hakeem, are collectively responsible for the charity’s governance, finances, safeguarding, strategy and compliance.

We will maintain clear financial separation between Christian faith and mission funding, community and civil-grant funding, humanitarian funding, restricted grants, missionary-support funds and church-development funds.

Our global vision

A trusted international Kurdish Christian network.

Headquartered in Liverpool, we hope to develop partnerships among Kurdish Christian communities in the UK, Europe, Iraq and the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, Syria, the United States and other countries where legitimate charitable needs exist.

International work will be developed gradually and responsibly, with appropriate local partnerships and governance and, when appropriate, legally independent partner organisations in other countries.
