How international aid will be protected

International humanitarian projects will be subject to strict due diligence, sanctions screening, financial controls and monitoring.

Due diligence

Verified local partners with clear governance.

Sanctions screening

Screening of partners, suppliers and payment routes.

Financial controls

Budgets, receipts and staged disbursement.

Monitoring

Reporting, verification and learning after every project.
